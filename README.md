# CHARTING A COURSE FOR MWANGAZA FILM STUDIO

## Business Problem
Mwangaza Film Studio is a new venture by a parent company seeking to enter the movie industry by creating original content. 
The company lacks experience in film production and seeks data-driven guidance on what types of movies perform best at the box office.

## Data
All data is located in the zippedData folder and includes:

- tn.movie_budgets.csv:  
  Source: The Numbers  
  Description: Contains production budgets and release dates for films.

- bom.movie_gross.csv.gz:  
  Source: Box Office Mojo  
  Description: Provides domestic and worldwide gross earnings.

- im.db.zip:  
  Source: IMDb  
  Description: Contains movie_basics and movie_ratings tables for film genres, runtimes, and IMDb ratings.

  ## Objectives
1. *Determine Market Viability*  
   - Assess which types of films resonate most with audiences and what factors contribute to commercial success.**
2. *Guide Strategic Decision-Making*  
   - Provide recommendations on which genres, styles, or storytelling approaches the new company should pursue.**
3. *Assess Optimal Runtime*  
   - *To determine the optimal range of movie runtime that is most often associated with high box office success and use it to give insights on the length of an ideal movie.

## Recommendations and Keys take aways
- Genre strategy recommendation: Invest 60% of the development budget of the top genres to ensure high audience appeal and box office returns
- Runtime Optimization Recommendation: Develop scripts optimized for the ideal timeframe to align with audience prefrence
- Budget vs financial success: prioritize funding for high budget films has proven to give maximum ratings and revenue if you keep into account other key factors such as story telling, marketing strategies and high quality captivating visuals.
